Can we estimate the panel orientation from the difference between Normal Irradiance peak hour and PPV peak hour?

The PPV curve is consistently 'skinner' than the irradiance curve.
Module efficiency tends to decrease at lower irradiance. This can make the beginning and end of the daily PV curve fall away. 
This is known as low-irradiance loss.
The NREL/Sandia PVWatts model: For POA/effective irradiance above 125 W/m^2, the simplified model is approximately linear: P_mp \prop E_e.
But below 125 W/m^2 P_mp \prop E_e^2
Introducing the efficiency due to irradiance coefficient, and multiplying POA by this coefficient, results in a corrected POA that much more closely matches the PPV curve.

However, the real PPV curves often have a bit of a weird skew to the left or right. (Time-dependent asymmetry).
